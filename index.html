Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What if the venue internet drops?

We test the connection in advance and discuss a backup hotspot plan. Hotspot availability, signal and data limits vary by location; no internet backup can be guaranteed.

Who controls privacy settings?

You choose the audience and privacy settings on your Facebook page, profile or YouTube channel. We can help you review the setup, but you remain in control of the account and audience.

What Facebook or YouTube permissions do I need?

The event host needs to grant the appropriate page or profile access for the planned stream, and must be able to start a Facebook Live or a YouTube Live (new YouTube channels may need up to 24 hours to enable live streaming, so set it up early). We confirm permissions together during the test run.

Will I get a recording?

A saved recording and one highlight clip are included with Premium. Recording availability can depend on the platform and the connection; ask before booking if a saved copy is essential.

Do you provide the venue internet?

We bring the camera and Android streaming setup. The venue or host should provide access to a reliable connection where possible; we assess options during the test run.

How do I reserve a date?

First confirm the date and package with us. A 50% deposit is required to book, paid through the Book buttons above.
